# Sale Items snapshot their own product name and price

Products carry drifted, inconsistent names accumulated over 8-9 years of VAT-machine registrations (e.g. "WATER PROOF MINI" and "WATER PROOF" both later folded into a single "WATER PRUF" catalog entry), and pricing is set at sale time under free-market conditions rather than fixed by the catalog. If `sale_items` only referenced `products.name`/`products.unit_price_before_vat`, every historical receipt would silently change whenever the admin renamed or repriced a Product — corrupting already-issued VAT receipts and reporting history.

Decided: `sale_items` stays linked to `products` via `product_id` (so Machine Code, unit, and per-product rollups still work), and its existing `description`/`unit_price` columns are treated as the snapshot — defaulted from the Product at creation and freely editable by the seller (mobile) or during backfill import (admin), rather than always mirroring the Product silently. Reporting and VAT export always read the Sale Item's own `description`/`unit_price`, never the Product's current values. Editing a Product's catalog name/price is purely a going-forward default change — it never retroactively touches past sales.
